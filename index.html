RESEARCH PROTOCOL

Out-of-sample discipline

Split observations chronologically. Fit or select parameters only on the training window; report the untouched test window separately. Walk-forward evaluation reruns the strategy on rolling windows. Historical optimisation can overfit noise and does not establish future profitability.

Metric definitions

CAGR — annualised geometric growth from start to end equity.

Sharpe — mean periodic excess return divided by periodic standard deviation, annualised by √252 (daily), √52 (weekly) or √12 (monthly).

Sortino — mean periodic return divided by downside deviation, annualised using the same frequency factor.

Maximum drawdown — minimum of equity / prior running peak − 1.

Profit factor — gross positive trade P&L divided by absolute gross negative trade P&L.

Execution convention — signals use completed bar data; orders execute at the next bar open. Stops/targets are checked on subsequent bars, with conservative stop-first handling when both are touched.
